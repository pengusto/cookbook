THE RECIPE

Less guesswork.
Better ingredients.

A checklist is a starting point. The skill first decides which checks apply to your product and target markets.

01

Know the dish.

Operator, markets, business model, audience. A contact form and a subscription app need different reviews.

02

Check the kitchen.

Trace SDKs, data flows, checkout, consent and claims. Compare the documents with what the app actually does.

03

Taste, then fix.

Get findings with evidence, current primary sources and observable checks. Ask for fixes when you want implementation.
